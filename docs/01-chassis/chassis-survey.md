# Gateway Chassis Survey

## Status

**In Progress**

## Purpose

The chassis survey determines whether the legacy Gateway enclosure can safely and practically support the modern hardware required for NUBLAR-CORE.

No major internal hardware should be purchased until this survey is complete.

## Known Chassis Identification

The rear factory label identifies the enclosure/system as:

- **Gateway Essential 500**
- **Label designation:** `LP MINI TOWER TB3 Essential 500`
- **Label date:** `11/20/99`

The unique factory serial/asset identifier is intentionally omitted from public documentation.

## Survey Principles

- Measure before buying.
- Preserve useful original mounting structures where practical.
- Avoid irreversible modification until compatibility options are understood.
- Cooling and serviceability take priority over exterior decoration.
- Do not assume standard ATX compatibility simply because the enclosure resembles a standard tower.

## Exterior Modification Constraints

Jurassic-inspired cosmetic changes must preserve:

- Air intake
- Exhaust
- Existing side-panel perforations unless intentionally redesigned
- Side-panel removal
- Front-panel operation
- Cable access
- Maintenance access
- Safe clearances around electrical components

3D-printed dinosaur skeletal elements must not obstruct ventilation or make routine maintenance impractical.
